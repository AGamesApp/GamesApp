Privacy Policy

AGames
Last Updated: December 11, 2025

Welcome to AGames, a mobile application that provides access to retro-style games through categorized emulation-based sections. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding data usage and permissions.

1. No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from users. This includes:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Contact lists
  • Photos or videos
  • GPS/location data
  • Call logs or messages
Your usage of AGames is completely anonymous.

2. File Storage

  • Downloaded game files are stored in your device's standard Downloads folder.
  • Users can manually delete downloaded files at any time through their device's file manager.
  • The app does not access any unrelated files or directories outside of the Downloads folder.
  • No files are stored in the app's private directory.

3. In-App Advertising

AGames displays ads using Google AdMob, which may include:

  • Banner ads
  • Interstitial ads
  • Rewarded video ads
  • Native (inline) ads

AdMob may collect anonymous data such as your device advertising ID to personalize or serve non-personalized ads. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

4. Dynamic Content from API

All content in the app (game titles, images, etc.) is loaded dynamically from a remote API.

  • The app fetches only metadata for display and functionality.
  • No personal data is transmitted to our servers.

5. Internet Usage

Internet access is used only for:

  • Loading game data dynamically from the API.
  • Displaying advertisements.
  • Checking for updates.

The app does not communicate with any other external servers or services.

6. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be communicated through the app or the store listing.
