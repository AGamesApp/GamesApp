IMPORTANT: DNS CONFIGURATION REQUIRED
================================================================================

To ensure your emails are delivered and not marked as spam, you MUST add
these DNS records in your Spaceship registrar:

1. SPF RECORD (TXT)
   -----------------
   Host: @ (or games.agames.cv)
   Type: TXT
   Value: v=spf1 a mx ip4:152.53.206.21 ~all
   
   This tells receiving mail servers that your IP is authorized to send
   emails for games.agames.cv

2. DMARC RECORD (TXT)
   -------------------
   Host: _dmarc (or _dmarc.games.agames.cv)
   Type: TXT
   Value: v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:admin@games.agames.cv
   
   This provides email authentication policy and reporting

3. MX RECORD (Optional but recommended)
   --------------------------------------
   Host: @ (or games.agames.cv)
   Type: MX
   Priority: 10
   Value: games.agames.cv
   
   This tells mail servers where to deliver emails for @games.agames.cv

4. PTR/rDNS RECORD (Recommended)
   ------------------------------
   Contact Hetzner support to set reverse DNS (PTR) for 152.53.206.21
   to point to: games.agames.cv
   
   This improves email deliverability and reduces spam scores

================================================================================
HOW TO ADD DNS RECORDS IN SPACESHIP
================================================================================

1. Log in to Spaceship (https://www.spaceship.com)
2. Go to "Domains" → Select "games.agames.cv"
3. Click on "DNS" or "DNS Records"
4. Click "Add Record" for each record above
5. Enter the values exactly as shown
6. Save changes
7. Wait 5-15 minutes for propagation
