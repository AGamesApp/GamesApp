================================================================================
THE PROBLEM
================================================================================

When Gmail receives your email, it does these checks:

1. ✅ SPF Check: "Is 152.53.206.21 allowed to send for games.agames.cv?" → YES (PASS)
2. ✅ DMARC Check: "Does this follow DMARC policy?" → YES (PASS)
3. ❌ rDNS Check: "Does 152.53.206.21 point back to games.agames.cv?" → NO (FAIL)
   
   Current: 152.53.206.21 → v2202512320709413627.quicksrv.de ❌
   Expected: 152.53.206.21 → games.agames.cv ✅

Gmail marks it as spam because the hostname doesn't match!
