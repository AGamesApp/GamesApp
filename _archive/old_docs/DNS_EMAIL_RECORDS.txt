================================================================================
DNS RECORDS FOR EMAIL - COPY & PASTE GUIDE
================================================================================

Domain: games.agames.cv
Add these records in Spaceship DNS Manager

================================================================================
RECORD 1: SPF (Required)
================================================================================

Type:     TXT
Host:     @
Value:    v=spf1 a mx ip4:152.53.206.21 ~all
TTL:      3600 (or Auto)

Purpose: Authorizes your server to send emails from @games.agames.cv


================================================================================
RECORD 2: DMARC (Required)
================================================================================

Type:     TXT
Host:     _dmarc
Value:    v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:admin@games.agames.cv
TTL:      3600 (or Auto)

Purpose: Email authentication policy and reporting


================================================================================
RECORD 3: MX (Optional but Recommended)
================================================================================

Type:      MX
Host:      @
Priority:  10
Value:     games.agames.cv
TTL:       3600 (or Auto)

Purpose: Tells mail servers where to deliver incoming emails


================================================================================
HOW TO ADD IN SPACESHIP (STEP BY STEP)
================================================================================

1. Go to: https://www.spaceship.com
2. Log in to your account
3. Click "Domains" in the menu
4. Click on "games.agames.cv"
5. Click "DNS" or "DNS Management"
6. Click "Add Record" button

FOR SPF RECORD:
  - Select Type: TXT
  - Host/Name: @ (or leave blank)
  - Value/Content: v=spf1 a mx ip4:152.53.206.21 ~all
  - Click Save

FOR DMARC RECORD:
  - Select Type: TXT
  - Host/Name: _dmarc
  - Value/Content: v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:admin@games.agames.cv
  - Click Save

FOR MX RECORD:
  - Select Type: MX
  - Host/Name: @ (or leave blank)
  - Priority: 10
  - Value/Content: games.agames.cv
  - Click Save

7. Wait 5-15 minutes for DNS propagation


================================================================================
VERIFICATION COMMANDS
================================================================================

After adding records, verify with these commands on your server:

# Check SPF record
dig TXT games.agames.cv

# Check DMARC record
dig TXT _dmarc.games.agames.cv

# Check MX record
dig MX games.agames.cv

================================================================================
WHAT THESE RECORDS DO
================================================================================

SPF (Sender Policy Framework):
  - Prevents email spoofing
  - Tells receiving servers: "152.53.206.21 is authorized to send mail for games.agames.cv"
  - Reduces spam score

DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication):
  - Provides authentication policy
  - Sends reports to admin@games.agames.cv about email delivery
  - Works with SPF to validate emails

MX (Mail Exchange):
  - Tells servers where to deliver incoming mail for @games.agames.cv
  - Not strictly required for sending, but good practice

rDNS/PTR (Reverse DNS):
  - Maps IP back to domain name
  - Many mail servers check this
  - Critical for deliverability

================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

If emails still go to spam:
  - Check DNS propagation (wait longer)
  - Verify SPF record is correct
  - Check rDNS is set
  - Test deliverability at: mail-tester.com

If DNS records not showing:
  - Clear DNS cache: sudo systemd-resolve --flush-caches
  - Check with different DNS: dig @8.8.8.8 TXT games.agames.cv
  - Wait longer (can take up to 48h, usually <1h)
